Method

How it was run

  1. Build

    Compiled 13,099 corporate contact records as the prospect base.

  2. Cold email

    Cold emails introducing the full investment banking offer, sent in daily batches from 12 Aug 2026.

  3. Screen

    Logged every batch, flagged invalid emails, and screened out prospects that were not eligible.

  4. Qualify

    Followed up responders and qualified the ones with a real capital need into M&A, tokenization and IPO opportunities.
